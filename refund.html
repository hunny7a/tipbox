Refund & Cancellation Policy

BAT V GAMING · Last updated: November 2026

All tips are final

Tips sent through this page are voluntary and non-refundable. Once your payment is made, the money goes directly to the Creator and your name and message are made available for display on the stream.

1. No refunds

A tip is considered delivered as soon as the payment is received and your name and message are displayed (or queued for display) on the stream. After that, it cannot be cancelled or refunded by this page.

2. Accidental payments and typing errors

Please check the amount, your name and your message before you pay. We cannot guarantee a refund for a wrong amount, a mistyped name or message, or a payment sent by mistake.

3. Technical errors

If you were charged twice for one tip, or your account was debited by a system error, email us as soon as possible at batvhelp@gmail.com with:

  • the date and time of payment,
  • the exact amount,
  • your UPI transaction ID (UTR), and
  • a screenshot of your payment confirmation.

The Creator will review the request and decide in good faith. Any return of funds is made by the Creator directly, outside this page.

4. Hidden or skipped messages

If your message is not displayed because it breaks the rules in the Terms, or because of filters or the Creator's decision, the tip is still final and not refundable.

5. Missing alerts

If your alert did not appear or was delayed, your payment is still valid. Alerts depend on internet and device conditions that are outside our control.

6. Disputes with your bank

Please contact us before raising a dispute with your bank or UPI app, so that we can try to resolve genuine problems quickly. Disputing a valid, completed tip after it has been displayed may be treated as misuse of this page.
