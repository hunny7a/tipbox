1. Ye page kya hai

Ye BAT V GAMING ke live streams ko support karne ke liye ek personal tip page hai. Tip dena poori tarah aapki apni marzi hai.

2. Payment kaise hota hai

Aapka paisa UPI se seedha creator ke UPI account mein jaata hai. Ye page bank ya payment company nahi hai. Hum aapka UPI PIN, OTP ya bank password kabhi nahi maangte. Koi maange to mat dena.

3. Refund Policy: tip non-refundable hai

Tip bhejne ke baad wo wapas nahi hoti. Isliye Tip bhejne se pehle box tick karwaya jaata hai. Wajah: tip aate hi stream par aapka naam aur message dikhne ka kaam ho jaata hai, aur paisa seedha creator ko chala jaata hai.

  • Galat amount ya galat naam/message: amount aur message bhejne se pehle dekh lo. Galti hone par bhi tip wapas hone ki guarantee nahi hai.
  • Technical galti (jaise ek hi tip ka paisa do baar kat gaya): turant email karo, saath mein payment ki date, time, amount aur UPI transaction ID (UTR) likho. Creator dekh kar apni marzi se faisla karega. Paisa lautana creator ke apne tareeke se hota hai.
  • Alert ya message nahi dikha: alert internet ya phone ke notification par chalta hai, kabhi kabhi der ho sakti hai ya na bhi dikhe. Isse payment ki sthiti nahi badalti.

4. Naam aur message

Aap jo naam aur message likhoge, wo stream par dikh sakta hai aur awaaz mein padha ja sakta hai. Gaali, nafrat, adult, dhamki, illegal ya doosron ki jaankari wale message mat bhejo. Creator aise message ko dikhane se mana kar sakta hai ya hata sakta hai. Tip phir bhi wapas nahi hoti.

5. Aapki jaankari

Hum sirf naam, message, amount aur time rakhte hain (email ya bank ki details nahi). Naam khali chhodne par aapka UPI wala naam (jo aapke payment notification mein aata hai) dikh sakta hai. Hatwane ki request email par bhejo.
